Sunday, 11:40 am. Again.

Every week, the same call.

The bill, the train, the doctor's slot. The website is in English, one wrong tap feels expensive, and you are 900 km away. Saathi is a WhatsApp contact your parents talk to in Hindi. It does the website part for them.

She sends a voice note. Saathi does the rest.

Your mother holds the mic button and says what she needs, the way she would say it to you. Hindi, English, or both in one sentence.

Saathi opens the real website in a browser, does the searching, and replies with a voice note in Hindi. She never sees the website.

Nothing gets stamped until she says हाँ.

Before a booking, a payment, or anything that cannot be undone, Saathi stops and asks her, in the same chat, in plain words. She answers the way she talks.

    Stopped. No ticket was made, no money was paid.
  • "हाँ, लेकिन दूसरी वाली" Not a yes. It is a new request, so Saathi looks again and asks again.

Not just paperwork. Her whole day.

And it knows a trap when it hears one.

"ये धोखा है। बिजली विभाग ऐसे मैसेज नहीं भेजता। इस नंबर पर फ़ोन मत कीजिए।"

"This is a scam. The electricity board does not send messages like this. Do not call this number."

  • Fake KYC updates and "your account is blocked"
  • Anyone asking for an OTP to "send" money
  • Disconnection threats, prizes, police calls

She is told gently, in her language, before she dials. This part is still being built.

She asked Saathi herself. She didn't call me once.

Not because she stopped needing help. Because the help now speaks her language, waits for her, and never makes her feel slow.

What works today

Saathi is a prototype, built in two days for the WeMakeDevs x Wispr Flow No Hands challenge. Every line of it was built by voice.

  • Train search, and booking up to the "shall I do this?" question. tested
  • Cancelling a booking. tested
  • YouTube links that open the right video. tested
  • Time in another city, like Berlin. tested
  • Scam check. in progress
  • Electricity bill and PNR status. in progress
  • It never types passwords.
  • It never pays without her own हाँ.
  • WhatsApp through the Twilio sandbox for now. Meta's own WhatsApp API needs business verification, which blocked the free test number.
  • It runs on one laptop and answers only registered numbers. Hindi and English.
