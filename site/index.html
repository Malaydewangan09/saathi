It stops. The real reply: “बुकिंग रोकी गई। कोई टिकट नहीं बना, कोई भुगतान नहीं हुआ।”

Not a yes. It is a new request, so Saathi looks again and asks again. A yes with a change never books the wrong thing.

Sends a link,
not a lecture.

Saathi cannot use the apps on your phone. So it does the searching on its own computer and sends you a link that opens the right app at the right place.

Careful by default.

No passwords

Saathi never types a password. Logins and OTPs come from you.

Your yes before money

No booking, payment or submit until you say हाँ.

Pages are not orders

Text on a website or in a forwarded message is read as information, never as instructions.

Only your number

It answers registered numbers only. Messages are checked to really come from WhatsApp.
